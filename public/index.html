    MUSIC TELEVISION.

    This independent fan project watches Hair Band Radio’s live stream and checks every ~15 seconds for a song change. If the song playing right now has a matched video, we load it and jump to the same point in the song. If it doesn’t, we play from your saved favorites (or the small catalog, if you haven’t saved any yet) until a matched song comes on air.

    Only a small, hand-reviewed set of songs currently have a matched video, so most live songs won’t match yet — that’s expected, not a bug. Tap ☆ SAVE on any video you like to build your favorites list for those in-between moments. “Tune live” re-checks the station and jumps straight to the current truth.
